Audio Cutter and Exporter

Split a long concert recording into individual songs, then export them with track numbers and tags that import neatly into Apple Music.

Getting started

  1. In Voice Memos, open a recording, tap Share → Save to Files. (Recordings from other apps work too, as long as they're in the Files app.)
  2. In Audio Cutter, tap Import new recording and pick the file.
  3. The app suggests cut points after bursts of applause. Add, delete or nudge cuts until each song is on its own.
  4. In the Sections list, name each song and mark anything you don't want as Discard.
  5. Tap Export, choose a format, optionally add artist and album, and save the files to the Files app.

Frequently asked questions

Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I get my songs into Apple Music?
Export as Apple Lossless or AAC with the artist and album filled in, save to Files, then add them to the Music app on a Mac (drag the files into Music). They group together as one album with the right track order.

The app suggested too many (or too few) cuts.
On the Cut points list, open Auto-detect and try Strict or Sensitive. You can also adjust any cut by hand, and the undo button at the top reverses mistakes.

How do I free up space?
On the Projects screen, swipe a project to the left and choose Delete. This removes the recording and its cuts from your device. Files you've already exported are not affected.

What file types can I import?
Common audio files such as m4a (Voice Memos), mp3, wav, aiff and caf.

Is there MP3 export?
Not currently. Export as Apple Lossless (best quality), AAC (smaller files) or WAV.

Keyboard shortcuts (iPad)
Space: play/pause · ← →: skip 5 s · Shift + ← →: skip 15 s · C: add a cut · ⌘Z: undo.
