Privacy Policy

Audio Cutter and Exporter · Last updated November 19, 2026

Audio Cutter and Exporter (“the app”) is built to work entirely on your device. This page explains what that means for your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your recordings and edits

  • When you import a recording, the app makes a copy inside its own private storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your cut points, section names and other project settings are saved on your device only.
  • Deleting a project in the app permanently removes its recording and settings from your device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Exporting

When you export, the app creates audio files and offers them through the standard iOS share sheet. Where those files go (for example, the Files app or AirDrop) is your choice. The app itself does not send them anywhere.

Network access and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet and contains no third-party code that collects data.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
